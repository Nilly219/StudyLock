Founder, StudyLock

I'm a high school student who builds things — StudyLock started as a way to solve a problem I kept running into myself: opening a new tab to look something up for homework, and somehow ending up on YouTube twenty minutes later.

I like building tools that are simple enough to actually stick with, rather than apps with more settings than substance.

Why I built this

Every focus app I tried either wasn't strict enough to matter, or was so complicated I stopped using it within a week. StudyLock is my attempt at the smallest version that actually works — a timer, a task list, and a lock that holds until time's up.
